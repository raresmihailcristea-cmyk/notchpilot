Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Does NotchPilot work on external screens or notch-less Macs?
Yes! It automatically detects notch-less displays and docks to the top bezel or floats as a desktop widget.

Q: How do I detach the HUD?
Click and drag the central textured grip handle. To re-dock, tap the "Reset to Notch" magnet button.

Q: How do I restore my lifetime purchase?
Click "Restore Purchases" in the Paywall or Settings window. StoreKit 2 will automatically sync with your Apple ID.
