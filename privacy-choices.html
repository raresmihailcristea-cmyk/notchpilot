Your Privacy Choices & Rights

NotchPilot Architecture: Zero-Data-Collection & 100% Local Processing

1. Tracking & Profiling Opt-Out

NotchPilot does not engage in cross-app tracking, analytics, or behavioral profiling. No tracking code or analytics SDKs exist in the binary.

2. Data Access & Deletion Requests

Because we do not collect or store any of your personal data on remote servers, there are no stored records to access, export, or delete. Your data never leaves your Mac.

3. Resetting Preferences

You can reset all local app preferences by running defaults delete com.notchpilot.app in Terminal or by uninstalling the application.
