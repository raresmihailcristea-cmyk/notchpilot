## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. Does NotchPilot work on Macs without a notch or external monitors?
**Yes.** NotchPilot automatically detects your monitor configuration. On external displays or notch-less Macs (such as iMac, Mac mini, Mac Studio, or older MacBooks), NotchPilot docks elegantly at the top-center bezel or can be detached anywhere on your desktop.

### 2. How do I move the HUD away from the notch?
Click and drag the central textured grip handle or the header bar of the expanded HUD. You can reposition it anywhere on your desktop. To snap it back to the notch, click the **"Reset to Notch"** magnet icon located at the top-right corner of the HUD.

### 3. What are the different display modes?
* **Hover Mode**: The HUD expands smoothly when your cursor hovers over the notch and retracts when your cursor leaves.
* **Stay Open Until Dismissed**: Expands on hover and stays open until you explicitly click away or close it.
* **Open All Time**: Remains pinned on screen as a constant hardware monitoring panel.

### 4. How does the 16-Day Free Trial work?
NotchPilot offers a fully unrestricted 16-day free trial from the first moment you launch the application. During the trial, all hardware telemetry features, process inspection tools, and customization themes are completely unlocked. Once the trial concludes, a one-time lifetime license ($7.99) can be unlocked via Apple In-App Purchase with zero recurring subscriptions.

### 5. How do I restore my purchase?
Open NotchPilot Preferences or click the Paywall window, then click **"Restore Purchases"**. StoreKit 2 will automatically verify your Apple ID receipt with Apple's servers.
