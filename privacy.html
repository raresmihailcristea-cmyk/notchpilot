Privacy Policy for NotchPilot

Last updated: September 8, 2026

NotchPilot ("the Application") is developed as a native macOS utility engineered with privacy as a foundational principle. This policy describes how your information is handled.

1. Zero Data Collection

NotchPilot does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data, usage metrics, analytics, crash logs, or unique device identifiers.

2. On-Device, Ephemeral Processing

All system metrics displayed—including CPU load, memory allocation, disk status, network bandwidth, and battery levels—are queried locally using official Apple system interfaces (Mach Kernel, BSD sockets, and Metal APIs).

  • These measurements reside exclusively in volatile memory (RAM).
  • Metrics are never written to disk and are never transmitted outside your Mac.

3. Network Interfaces

While the app reads the byte count on your active network interface to report upload and download speeds, it does not inspect packet contents, domain names, or internet traffic.

4. No Third-Party SDKs or Trackers

The application is built entirely with first-party Apple Swift and SwiftUI frameworks. It contains no analytics frameworks, no advertising SDKs, and no tracking scripts.

5. Apple App Sandbox Compliance

NotchPilot operates strictly within the secure macOS App Sandbox container, ensuring isolated execution and complete protection for your personal files and applications.
